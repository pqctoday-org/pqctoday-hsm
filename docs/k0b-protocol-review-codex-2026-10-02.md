Points on which two implementers would diverge:

- The exact evidence ASN.1 profile, claim identifiers and value types, ordering, role separation, and signed bytes.
- Device-ID derivation and equality rules among recovery, attestation, package-signing, and receipt-signing chains.
- Challenge issuance/session binding and transaction-ID generation and uniqueness scope.
- Reservation recovery before commit, cache eviction/full behavior, and authenticated tombstone retention after object deletion.
- Public/private pair linkage and the installed public object’s attributes and discovery path.
- Whether `maxReplicas` is source-local or lineage-global, plus the canonical provenance encoding and exact history values.
- Archival-time validation, historical CRL treatment, backup expiry, and recovery-key rotation continuity.
- Nested/aggregate parser, CRL, policy, cache, and snapshot limits.
